Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-02

Ouellet Studio ("we," "us") operates ouelletstudio.com and is based in Quebec, Canada. This page explains what personal information this site collects, why, and how it's handled. It applies to this website only, not to any delivered client work or private agreements, which are covered separately in your delivery terms.

What we collect

We collect personal information only when you submit it directly through one of two forms on this site:

  • Commission inquiry form — name, organization (optional), email address, phone number (optional), and the details of your inquiry (sector, hub, production tier, privacy preference).
  • Shadow Fleet access request — email address only.

We don't run analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies on this site. Standard web server logs (e.g. IP address, browser type) may be retained briefly by our hosting provider as part of normal operation, but we don't use them to identify or profile visitors.

Why we collect it

Solely to respond to your inquiry, evaluate a potential commission, or process a Shadow Fleet access request. We don't use this information for marketing, and we don't sell, rent, or trade it to anyone.

Who sees it

Form submissions are delivered to us through Formspree, a third-party form-processing service, which transmits the submission to our email address. Formspree processes the data solely to deliver it to us and is bound by its own privacy terms. No other third party receives this information.

How long we keep it

Inquiry and access-request submissions are kept only as long as needed to respond to you and, if a commission proceeds, for the duration of that working relationship and any applicable record-keeping period. You can ask us to delete information we hold about you at any time (see below).

Your rights

Under Quebec's private-sector privacy law, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information we hold about you, and to ask questions about how it's used. To exercise any of these, contact:

danick@ouelletstudio.com

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.

This page is provided for transparency and isn't a substitute for legal advice. If you have specific compliance questions, consult a qualified privacy lawyer.
