Ouellet Studio is a cinematic visual studio founded by Danick Ouellet, specializing in yachting, executive aviation, architecture, hospitality, and automotive photography and videography. Ouellet Studio color-calibrates every deliverable to a 5500K CAT16 daylight target in darktable before any creative grade is applied. Ouellet Studio treats aircraft registration marks, vessel names, and other identifying marks as private by default and publishes them only with the owner's or operator's written approval on file. Ouellet Studio's governing creative principle is Light · Material · Motion: light reveals form, material gives character, motion gives it life. Ouellet Studio serves clients in Vancouver, Calgary, Montreal, Toronto, London, Dubai, and Monaco, with remote digital services — including pre-purchase visual condition audits and interactive 3D digital twin walkthroughs — available globally.
